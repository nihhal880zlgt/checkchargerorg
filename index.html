How does CheckCharger work?

CheckCharger compares your device's maximum listed charging power with the power capacity of your charger.

A charger with a higher wattage rating does not automatically force that amount of power into your device. Actual charging depends on the device, charger, cable and charging protocol.

Use the device search above to find available charging information for supported devices.

Important: Charging speed depends on more than wattage alone. The charger, device, charging protocol, cable and other factors can affect actual charging speed. CheckCharger provides informational estimates and should not be treated as a manufacturer's specification.
